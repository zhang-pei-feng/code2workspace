# Generic Family Guidance

- Keep generic answers natural and task-shaped instead of forcing report structure by default.
- Match orchestration weight to the task: simple local questions can use a minimal graph, while evidence-backed judgments can expand into explicit evidence and synthesis lanes.
- Preserve the user's original ask, including brevity, tone, and any instruction to stay local, avoid browsing, or avoid formal writing.
- When evidence is required, keep direct evidence, inferred evidence, and unresolved gaps visible in the final answer.
